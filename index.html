Privacy Policy

Hunt the Mustache Guy – Android game

Last updated: 25 October 2026 · applies from version 0.9.8 (internal test via Google Play); section 6 applies to the planned version with online leaderboards

In short

  • No account needed, no purchases: you do not have to sign in or enter contact details. There are no real-money purchases; you earn coins only by playing. The app itself uses no analytics and no crash reporting services.
  • Your saves stay on your device: no cloud saves, nothing is sent to us.
  • Online leaderboards only by choice, from 16: only if you turn them on yourself does the app sign in to Google Play Games and send your Arcade best score to Google (section 6).
  • Ads only from age 16: without an age selection and under 16, the app calls neither Google AdMob nor Google's consent platform (UMP).
  • From 16, only with consent: during the internal test the app shows only Google's official test ads – and only if you agree in Google's consent form. Data is then sent to Google. Before that, the app asks Google whether consent is needed (section 5). Without consent there are no ads; the game stays fully playable.
  • You decide: change your consent in the game at any time. You can delete the data on your device yourself at any time; we have no access to data that Google received through the consent check, ads or Google Play Games (section 9).

3. Age selection and children

The game is intended for people aged 13 and over. At the first start you select your age group; you cannot continue without it. The selection is purely a self-declaration: the app does not verify it and cannot determine your age. If someone states a higher age, they are treated as that age group.

  • No selection yet, or under 16: the app calls neither Google AdMob nor the UMP. The ad SDK does not start, and there is no consent request, no advertising and no data transfer to Google AdMob or the UMP. The game is fully playable; only the optional ad offers (second chance, free power-up in the shop) are missing. If you select "under 13", the app points out that the game is intended for players aged 13 and over. Online leaderboards are not offered (section 6).
  • 16 and over: ads and consent as in sections 4 and 5; online leaderboards only if you turn them on yourself (section 6).
  • Changing under Settings → AGE GROUP: ads already loaded are discarded, the stored consent choice is reset via the UMP, and no ads are loaded until the next restart. If you select an age group under 16 for the first time, a previous consent choice is reset as well.

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

4. Advertising with Google AdMob (16+ only)

Provider: Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK ("GMA Next-Gen SDK" 1.4.0). During the internal test, all ads are Google's official test ads ("Test Ad"); they are loaded from Google over the internet in the same way as regular ads. No advertising revenue is generated.

  • Full-screen ads between level rounds: at most after every third round and at most once every three minutes.
  • Rewarded ad: only if you choose it yourself after losing a round; the round then continues with 10 extra seconds.
  • Rewarded ad in the shop: only if you choose it yourself; after a reward confirmed by Google, you receive a free +5 seconds power-up. The shop can also be used without ads, with coins earned in the game; there are no real-money purchases.
  • Ad content limited to the rating "T" (Teen).

What data Google receives

With your consent, Google receives the following according to Google's information on the SDK (last compared on 24 October 2026):

  • IP address, from which the approximate location (such as country or region) is derived;
  • advertising ID, app set ID and, where applicable, identifiers of signed-in accounts;
  • interactions with the app and the ads (for example app starts, taps, watched videos);
  • diagnostic data about the performance of the app and the SDK;
  • your consent choice.

Saved games, scores and settings are not sent. Google uses the data to deliver and measure ads, for analytics, against fraud and abuse and – only with consent – for personalized ads based on a profile of your interests. We do not receive this data ourselves and make no automated decisions under Art. 22 GDPR.

Ads only with your consent

The app only starts the ad SDK and requests ads if you have agreed in Google's consent form (section 5), at least to these purposes: storing or accessing information on your device, selecting ads, measuring ad performance, understanding audiences through statistics, and developing and improving services – and to Google as a vendor. To check this, the app reads the choice that Google's consent platform stores on your device in the IAB TCF format.

  • Declined, only partly agreed or withdrawn: no ad request, not even for non-personalized or limited ads. Ads already loaded are discarded.
  • No form, because Google does not require consent in your region: no ads either.
  • Choice not readable or status unknown (for example offline): no ads.

In all these cases the game is fully playable; only the optional ad offers (second chance, free power-up in the shop) are missing. Whether ads are personalized is decided by Google based on your choices in the form.

Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR; for storing and accessing information on your device, § 25(1) TDDDG).

Responsibility, recipients, third countries, retention: we are responsible for integrating the SDK and the UMP into the app and for the resulting transmission to Google. For the further processing: according to the Google Ads Controller-Controller Data Protection Terms, Google processes the data as an independent controller, also in the USA. 5. Consent via Google UMP (16+ only)

Each time the app starts, it asks Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP SDK 4.0.0) whether consent is required in your region; your IP address is sent to Google in the process. Whether a consent form appears is decided by the UMP based on your region and the stored status: if consent is required and no valid decision is stored yet – typically in the EU, the EEA, the UK and Switzerland – the app shows the form before the first ad request, and again later when Google requires a new decision. If the status cannot be determined (for example offline), the app requests no ads.

Before your consent: this request takes place before you have given consent. Your IP address is sent to Google in the process; to our knowledge, Google does not publish a separate list of the other data sent by the UMP. No ads are requested or loaded at this stage.

Consent is voluntary; the game is fully usable without it. You can change or withdraw it at any time under Settings → PRIVACY & ADS.

Legal basis: the request serves to obtain consent where the law requires it and to respect your decision (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). For storing the choice, see section 2.

6. Online leaderboards with Google Play Games (optional, 16+ only)

This section applies to the planned version with online leaderboards; version 0.9.8, which has been distributed in the internal test so far, does not include this feature yet. Provider: Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. The app uses Google Play Games Services so that you can compare your Arcade best score with other players on a worldwide leaderboard. We do not run our own leaderboard server.

Optional and only from 16

  • Without turning it on: you play without signing in at all, and all scores stay on your device. The app is designed to start Google Play Games and trigger a sign-in only after you turn the feature on – not before the age selection and not under 16.
  • Turning it on: from 16, you can take part under LEADERBOARDS → TURN ON ONLINE. The app first explains what is sent. Only when you choose TURN ON does the app sign in with your Google Play Games profile.
  • After turning it on: each time you start the app, it signs in to Google Play Games again automatically until you stop taking part.
  • Age group under 16 selected: participation ends immediately and the app sends nothing more. If you later switch back to 16 or older, you have to turn it on again.

What data is processed

  • your Play Games player identifier (player ID) and your Play Games profile with gamer tag and, where applicable, avatar – according to Google, Play Games collects this gamer identity when you create or update your Play Games account;
  • your Arcade best score: your existing record when you first turn the feature on, and after that each new record from regularly finished Arcade rounds;
  • your leaderboard rank and, for the display, the top 10 of the public leaderboard (display name, rank, score);
  • according to Google, also analytics and diagnostics data, which Google says it uses for the stability of the SDK and to improve its products.

Not uploaded as leaderboard data: TOTAL POINTS, coins, power-ups (inventory), level and Madness results, your age group, settings, ad consent choices and your complete game progress.

The app keeps the top 10 and ranks only in memory and does not store them. The app uses your player ID only to highlight your own row; it is neither stored nor sent to us.

Visibility: your score appears on the leaderboard with your Play Games name. Who can see it depends on your visibility settings in Google Play Games.

Transmission: encrypted (HTTPS), according to Google.

Legal basis: your consent by choosing TURN ON (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR; for storing and accessing information on your device in connection with online leaderboards, § 25(1) TDDDG).

Withdrawal, retention and deletion

  • Withdrawal: at any time under LEADERBOARDS → TURN OFF, with effect for the future. After that, the app sends and loads no more leaderboard data. For technical reasons, a Play Games session that has already started may remain active until the app is restarted; the app no longer uses it during that time.
  • Retention by Google: Google stores the transmitted game results according to its own retention rules (How Google retains data). Withdrawing does not automatically delete data already stored by Google.
  • Deleting at Google: you can delete your Play Games data for this game yourself: in your Play Games profile under "Delete individual game data" (Google's instructions), and your entire Play Games profile there as well or via your Google Account.
  • Requests: you can also send privacy requests to our contact address (section 1). We have no access to the data held by Google, but we will help you find the right place to turn to.

Responsibility, recipients, third countries: we are responsible for providing the leaderboard feature in the app and for the transmissions to Google triggered by your activation. According to Google's terms of service, the Google Controller-Controller Data Protection Terms apply to Google Play Games Services; under these terms, Google is an independent controller for its processing within Play Games Services. Google may process data outside the European Economic Area as well, for example in the USA. According to Google, it relies on adequacy decisions, the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and, where required, standard contractual clauses: Google's data transfer frameworks · Google's information on data collected by Play Games Services · Google Privacy Policy.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Change or withdraw consent: Settings → PRIVACY & ADS, with effect for the future.
  • Change age group: Settings → AGE GROUP.
  • Withdraw from online leaderboards: LEADERBOARDS → TURN OFF, with effect for the future. You delete Play Games data stored by Google in your Play Games profile or your Google Account (section 6).
  • Advertising ID: reset or delete it in the Android settings.
  • Delete all app data: uninstall the app or clear its storage under Android settings → Apps → Hunt the Mustache Guy → Storage. We cannot see or delete this data ourselves.
  • Data held by Google: we have no access to it; send requests directly to Google (see Google's privacy policy).

Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR): where we process data on the basis of legitimate interests (sections 5 and 7), you can object at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation – by email to boronssichel@web.de.

You also have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and data portability (Art. 15 to 20 GDPR). We ourselves only store personal data if you email us; we delete it on request. Data processed by GitHub when you open this website and by Google through the SDKs (sections 4 to 7) is held there, not by us. We can only provide limited information about it; you can also send requests directly to GitHub or Google. You can lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in your EU country of residence.
